# Grade the answer

1. **answers_question**
   - 1. The answer states that no alerts are firing.
   - 0. The answer does not say if alerts are firing, or it says that alerts are firing.
2. **is_short**
   - 1. The answer has one to three sentences and at most 40 words.
   - 0.5. The answer is correct but has more than 40 words.
   - 0. The answer is long or does not answer the question.
